# Shawnovision Pantheon Lore Bible

Love the energy—keeping it snappy, dandy, and rooted right in the Okanagan! ☀️🍷

Since your business motto is all about being handy and practical while keeping that local flair, we can easily weave that exact vibe into the Shawnovision Pantheon lore. Here are three quick ways to make this construct feel right at home in your worldbuilding:

# **1. The Okanagan Context: "The Redactor of Orchard Records"**

In the local archives of your world, this construct doesn't just redact vague historical grief. It roams the dusty basements of old packing houses, estate wineries, and missing land registries.

**The Snappy Lore:** It redacts the tragic years of the "Great Frost" or the bitter family feuds over property lines, replacing them with sarcastic footnotes like, "Yes, the vineyard failed. No, it wasn't the soil, you just forgot to water it."

# **2. A Handy Encounter Idea (The "Dandy" Merchant)**

Instead of a scary monster, make it a hyper-useful, slightly annoying neutral NPC your players or characters encounter.

**How it helps (Handy):** It can decipher any ancient, water-damaged map or contract instantly.

**The Catch (Snappy):** It will format the answer as a brutal corporate memo, and the players only have 30 seconds to memorize it before the spectral ink vanishes into gold glitter.

# **3. Expanding the Pantheon (A Companion Construct)**

To keep the momentum going for your daily lore expansion, every "Records Keeper" needs a field technician. You could introduce a companion construct that matches your business motto perfectly:

**The Builder of Solace:** A clockwork automaton made of old apple crates and weathered barn wood. While the Redactor deletes the bad stuff with snark, this one follows behind to clumsily fix things up, hammering glowing protective brackets onto broken structures.
